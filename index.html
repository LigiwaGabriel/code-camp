Sunrise Bakery has been serving our neighbourhood since 2015 with scratch-made bread, seasonal pastries, and custom cakes for every occasion.

About our bakery

Every loaf and pastry is prepared by hand in our small kitchen using local flour, butter, and fruit whenever we can source it. We open at 6:30am so early risers can pick up something warm on the way to work.
